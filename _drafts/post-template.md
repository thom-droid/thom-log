Write two or three sentences about the problem or question that led to this
post. Give the reader enough context to understand why the lesson matters.

## What I learned

State the central idea in your own words. Prefer a small, concrete explanation
over a broad survey of the topic.

## Example

```text
Add the smallest useful code, command, error message, or example here.
```

Explain what the example demonstrates and call out the detail that was easy to
miss.

## What went wrong

Optional: record the failed approach, misleading assumption, or debugging clue.
This is often the part that will be most valuable when you return later.

## Takeaway

Summarize the lesson in one to three bullets:

- What should I remember?
- When should I use it?
- What should I investigate next?
